The problem

AI starts building before it knows what you want.

You type an idea into the terminal — the text window where AI writes code. It guesses the details and builds right away. You get something else, and you can't read the code to fix it.

The solution

See the plan before any code is written.

turns your idea into a flowchart anyone can read. You comment, change and approve it. Only what you approved gets built.

How it works

Three steps. No code.

1

Describe

Tell the AI what you want, in plain words — like you'd tell a friend.

2

Review

Check the flowchart. Click any box to ask a question or change it.

3

Approve

Hit approve. The AI builds exactly that plan — nothing more, nothing less.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You read a flowchart, not code. The AI does the coding.

Isn't planning slower than just building?

A minute of planning saves hours of "that's not what I meant". You only build once.

What if I change my mind later?

Add a comment to the plan. It becomes a new version, and nothing is built until you approve again.
